# 2.0 - Prologue

*The Guild locked you behind iron under the mountain. You left over the falls. Orgrim stayed.*

## 2.0.1: The Cells

The last clear thing was Yennen's coin ringing on the sanctum floor. After it came a hood, hands on your arms, a wet cloth over the mouth and a smell like scorched honey. You held your breath for as long as a body can. Then you woke on cold stone, each in your own cell, in rock that somebody had carved long ago with knotwork and angular letters and that centuries had worn almost flat. An iron door with no handle. A hatch at its foot, shut. It took a while to work out how you had been divided: **Gunnar** and **Brawn** in one row with **Orgrim** and **Grukk One-Tusk**, **Riven** and **Hanzt** in another with **Harald** and **Balder**. Sorted by race, or by somebody's best guess at it.

You heard **Tarod** before you saw him. He dragged Vorgra along the iron doors, Gunnar's own weapon, and banged it into whatever it met so that nobody would be asleep when he started talking. He had something for each of you. For Brawn: *"Five years in a cell and you found peace. Let's see if it holds in this one."* For Hanzt: *"Pathogen. You cut the braid and shaved the beard. You still flinch the same."* For Gunnar: *"Too much Ork for the Elves, too much Elf for the Orks. We put you with the Orks. Somebody had to guess."* He admired the weapon in his hand while he said it. *"A bat wrapped in wire. They raised you well."* For Riven: *"Fine bow. Tyr's work, I am told. It will fetch more than you ever would have."*

**Yennen** came with him, once to each row, and he had not come to gloat. He begged. He apologised and then asked again: give up Hammerfall, let the Guild have it, please. He still would not say who had sent him. Harald and Balder heard him out and gave him no answer he could carry back. When he had gone, Balder told the boy what he thought. Let the city go. Come home to Yorvik, where Balder could keep him safe until he was older.

The other row gave him less. Orgrim called Yennen a snake and a coward and said he regretted ever letting him sit beside him on the Pact. Neither Ork wanted Hammerfall for its own sake. They would not be seen handing it over.

> "Yoooou picked a poor place to beg, little man."
>
> Grukk One-Tusk

Then there were the doors. Gunnar had woken in the pitch dark with his tongue swollen and no voice. Under the mouldy hay in the corner of his cell he found a mouse hole, and as a rat he went through it into the cell next door. Nobody was held there. Its iron door had melted shut in the frame, and a skeleton lay against the back wall with a cracked mirror in its left hand and soot burned around it like a shadow. A hole an inch wide looked through into Brawn's cell. Too small for a rat. Wide enough to see through, and for Gunnar and his own mirror, seeing a place was enough. On the far side Brawn had found that his door had been altered, a fitted plate across the top held by two diamond-shaped bolts. His chain hand closed around them like a spanner. With the plate off there was a gap above the door, a few fingers high. A rat's worth.

In the other row the Guild had left Hanzt drugs. He put them to work, with sulphur and coal residue scraped from Riven's cell and passed through the holes in the wall, the oil from his own unlit torch, and straw and cloth from the bed for a fuse. Riven's door carried a weld where it had once been repaired, which marked it as the weak one. The charge went back through the wall to him. His amulet swallowed the noise. Then Eirunn went through what was left of the door.

When both rows were out, Yennen told you of a secret way. Your gear was stashed for you, he said, at a place where the tunnels widen and a company can rest. He would meet you there. He sent you into the dark in two groups, by separate ways.

## 2.0.2: The Oasis

The tunnels widened where Yennen had said they would. Three passages met on one side of the chamber. On the other lay a small rock pool, black and still, with benches cut into the walls and barrels in the corner, and a vast relief stretched across the far wall, lost in shadow. The Ork row came in by one passage and the Norse row by another, and the company was whole again.

The barrels held everything. Vorgra. Tyr's Measure. Armour, packs, coin, down to the last bent nail. You ate, bound your wounds and slept in turns on the stone benches.

Yennen did not come.

Orgrim would not sit. *"The fat dwarf sold us once. We go now. Nothing the Guild has down here will hold against us."* Balder did not get up. *"He had no reason to bring us here. Less reason to give it all back. He's earned some time."* Harald nodded.

Then voices came up the third passage, and Yennen burst in with three Guild soldiers at his back. Swords came out. One of the three was quicker than the others and better prepared. A single rapier thrust went through the throats of both his comrades, and their shouts of surprise ended in a gurgle of blood. He sheathed the blade with a flourish and dipped into a deep, elegant bow before your faces. This was **Aran**, a Guild soldier who had defected with Yennen.

Yennen cut his introduction short. *"Yes, yes, Aran, we have no time for this. Our defection will only go unnoticed briefly. He is old and cunning. We have a moment's advantage, no more."* He took a torch to the relief, and in its light the carving came alive: dozens of small creatures at play in the water. Some were unmistakably dwarves. The others were smaller, furred, with teeth and claws and thick tails. Then Yennen shrank. Brown fur, webbed feet, a thick muscular tail, whiskers crowding a short blunt nose. The torch dropped and Aran picked it up. The otter slipped into the pool and was gone. A moment later the relief split down the middle and ground apart onto a narrow passage, and wet air spilled out of it with a distant roar of water.

You followed Aran's torch up over wet rock and cracked stone until the light ahead stopped being torchlight. Daylight. At the far end of a vast cave the rock opened to the sky, and a river poured over the edge into a wall of white mist. The light fell on an island with black water running round it in narrow channels and out over the falls: palm trees, ferns, moss and grass, a clear spring under an overhang of rock. The place on the wall. At its heart hung a great golden gong, still swinging from the strike.

The sound hit. It rolled into every tunnel and off every wall, and deep in the mountain behind you it was still going. Three Guild soldiers stood beneath the gong. One of them held Yennen, dripping and back in his own shape, with a blade at his throat, and grinned at you across the water. *"He is coming! You are all doomed!"* The blade bit. Orgrim did not say a word. He charged, cleared the gap and landed on the island, and the threat was still hanging in the air when the doomhammer came down on the soldier's skull.

The two spearmen beside him fell fast. Nothing after them did. Otters came up out of the channels, and warriors in heavy armour came out of the tunnel mouths with wooden bridges to throw across the water. Aran did not make for the island at all, so Brawn picked him up and ran him across the water to safety. The otters bit Aran anyway, and he has hated otters ever since. You slipped in among the warriors, unhooked the west and east bridges without one of them landing a blow, and threw both into the deep river.

From the moment the gong rang there was a voice inside your skull. *I am coming... for... you.*

The south bridge still stood.

## 2.0.3: The Falls

The footsteps came from all three passages. Then gold caught the torchlight. Three figures filled the passage mouths, west, east and the tunnel that had brought you here: hammered plates, broad shoulders, runes cut into gold. Golden golems. They reached the water and did not stop. One heavy foot went under, then another, and black water rose over the engraved plates until the faces vanished. For a moment only the water moved. Then a golden hand closed around the island's edge.

Two of them were slow. One walked the river bottom and the other spent its time hauling itself up onto the island. The third came over the south bridge, the one you had left standing, and swung the moment it arrived.

Yennen held you up. He threw a ward over every one of you at once and laid healing hands on whoever was going down. Some of the warriors and otters fell. More kept coming, and the golems shrugged off nearly everything you put into them.

Brawn saw the way out first: the river, the edge, the falls. He talked you into it one at a time. Harald called a fighting retreat, and the company broke away across the island without being cut down from behind. Most of you got clear.

Orgrim and Grukk did not. Orks do not run from a foe, and their fighting retreat was all fighting, against the numbers and against every one of you begging them to come. Tarod showed his face long enough to twist the knife. *"The mighty Orks flee."*

The voice came again, and this time it spoke of Yennen. He had always known Yennen was weak, the voice said, and always known he would turn. He had been too kind to his nephew. That was a mistake he would now correct.

Then the southern passage burst, and the voice you had carried since the gong had a shape: huge, scaled, and all malice. Yennen was shepherding the last of you toward the edge, still pleading with the Orks, already changing into an otter. He did not finish. The jaws took him mid-change, and his magic snapped.

Grukk bared his teeth and set himself to charge. Orgrim got there first. He roared and cannoned into him, and knocked him into the water toward the edge of the falls.

> "You will not fall today, old man. This is not your fight. He is mine."
>
> Orgrim

The river took you. White water filled your ears and your mouth, and the falls drowned everything else. From the lip you saw Orgrim alone on the island, bringing the doomhammer to bear, and the blow glancing off gold-edged scale. Then cold, gravelly laughter.

*"Your hammer is but a pebble afore my landslide..."*

The river tipped you over the edge.

## Remember For Next Time

- **Yennen:** Dead. He defected, gave you back your gear, held you up at the oasis and was taken while he was getting you out.
- **Orgrim:** He put Grukk in the river and stayed on the island alone with the doomhammer.
- **He:** The one the Guild said was coming, and the voice in your heads. Huge and scaled. Yennen was his nephew.
- **Aran:** A Guild soldier who defected with Yennen and now fights beside you. He hates otters.
- **Hammerfall:** The Guild wanted it given up. Harald never agreed.

## Where We Are

*Over the falls and out of Hreidmar: all of you, with Eirunn, Harald, Balder and Grukk. Orgrim stayed on the island.*
