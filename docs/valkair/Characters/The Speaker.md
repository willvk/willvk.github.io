# The Speaker
*Blood Elf representative of [[The Chosen]]. Dead.*

The Speaker spoke for the Chosen on [[The Pact]]. He handled the catacombs, temples, worship, and the rituals that kept the Blood Elf presence in Hammerfall organized. He was the clearest public face of the cult.

The face was a disguise. After [[Njord]]'s drowned temple he told the party what they had done: the girl they freed from the ruby portal was [[Hannah]]'s imprisoned soul, and freeing her had set her rebirth in motion. Then he shed the disguise, and the Blood Elves fell on Hammerfall.

The party killed him that night, standing with Harald, [[Orgrim]], Grukk One-Tusk and [[Yennen]]. On his body was a book bound in claw. It gave them five nights.
