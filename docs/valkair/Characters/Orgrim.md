# Orgrim
*Orgrim Doomhammer. Chieftain of all the Orks. Pact representative.*

Orgrim leads the united Ork tribes and spoke for them on [[The Pact]]. He allied with [[Ivar]] to form the Great Heathen Army and helped take [[Hammerfall]] from the elves. In the council chamber he sat vast and still in his prime, with the skull-faced doomhammer across his knees.

He was one of the strongest faces of Ork power in the city, responsible for guards, city defense, and keeping the Pact from turning on itself. When it turned anyway, he stood with the party against [[The Speaker]] and held the chamber until dawn.

He walked into the ruined sanctum after [[Hannah]] died and took each of the party by the forearm, hard enough to bruise. A minute later [[The Guild]]'s soldiers came with nets and clubs.

In the cells [[Yennen]] came begging for the city. Orgrim does not want Hammerfall for its own sake. He will not be seen handing it over. He called Yennen a snake and a coward, and said he regretted ever letting him sit beside him on the Pact. [[Grukk One-Tusk]] was in the same row of cells, and agreed.
