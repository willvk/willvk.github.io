# Yennen
*aka Trader Yennen. Once [[The Guild]]'s man in Hammerfall.*

Yennen was a fat, amiable dwarf from the outer lands. He sat on [[The Pact]] for the Guild and kept the city's trade, money, and practical upkeep moving. He was friendly on the surface and very difficult to ignore when money was involved.

He stood with the party against [[The Speaker]]. Then, with [[Hannah]] newly dead, he stepped forward sweating, with Guild soldiers behind him and no scroll in his hand, and told the survivors that Hammerfall passed into Guild administration that night. The party was to be taken alive, all of them. He would not say whose instruction that was. *"Nobody do anything... expensive."* A Guild coin slipped from his pocket in the sanctum. It spun on the stone for a moment and was still.

He came to the cells afterwards, and he did not come to gloat. He apologised and he begged: give up Hammerfall, let the Guild have it, please. When the party broke out he told them of a secret way through the tunnels, and promised their gear would be waiting where the passage widens.

It was. Every piece of it, in the barrels, down to the last bent nail. Yennen came late, with three Guild soldiers at his back, and one of them was Aran, who had defected with him and killed the other two with a single rapier thrust before their shouts of surprise were out. Yennen had no time for introductions. *"Our defection will only go unnoticed briefly. He is old and cunning."* He took a torch to the carving on the wall, dwarves and otters at play in the water, and then he became one of the otters. He slipped into the pool, and a moment later the wall split open.

The Guild caught him at the oasis beyond, beneath the gong, with a blade at his throat. [[Orgrim]] freed him. From then on Yennen held the company up: a ward over every one of them at once, healing hands on whoever was going down, while the golems came out of the water.

He was the nephew of the one the Guild said was coming. The voice told them so before it came through the wall. Yennen spent his last moments shepherding the company toward the falls and pleading with the Orks to run, and he was halfway into an otter again when the jaws took him. His magic snapped.
