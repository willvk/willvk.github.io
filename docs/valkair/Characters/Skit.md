# Skit
*Translator to [[Vress]].*

Young and quick, Skit stood at Vress's shoulder in the Matriarch's Hall and translated for her, quick enough to edit as he goes. He was there when [[Odin]]'s sanctum opened.

When the party brought the kobolds' mechanism back where it belonged, Skit nearly bounced out of his skin, then rushed them to the engineers so they could all talk over one another in their excitement. Six of them swarmed Hanzt's crossbow, and it came back as [[Storm's Essence]].
