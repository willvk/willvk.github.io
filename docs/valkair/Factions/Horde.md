Warhorde of Orks from the southern Middens and generally south of [[Hammerfall]], led by [[Orgrim|Orgrim Doomhammer]].

Orgrim allied the Horde with [[Ivar]]'s Norse to form the Great Heathen Army, and together they took Hammerfall from the elves. Under [[The Pact]] the Ork warbands held their share of [[Upper Hammerfall]] and kept the guards and the city's defence. [[Grukk One-Tusk]] is one of Orgrim's elite. When the Pact broke, the Orks pulled inward and trusted nothing but their old traditions.
