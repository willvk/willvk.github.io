[[The Guild]] is predominantly compiled of Norseman but pays no attention to race or creed and as such the Guild is a force unmatched. The power of coin controls the fate of many and The Guild controls the coin. A brutal organisation of individuals, disputes are common but The Guild handles their own disagreements brutally and violently. There is a saying in the Guild, though the face may change, the price does not. This explains the nature of the Guild as the enormity of the organisation is hard to fathom and their power attracts many types; merchant, pirate or mercenary. True power within The Guild is fleeting as wealth and territory changes hands often between its members but to represent The Guilds interests in Hammerfall they appointed [[Yennen]], a fat amiable dwarf from the outer lands. Not much is known about Yennen or what his motivations are in Hammerfall but you can be sure of one thing, a price must always be paid.

## Hammerfall

The price came due at the end of Act 1. With [[The Pact]] in ruins and [[Hannah]] newly dead, Yennen declared that Hammerfall passed into Guild administration, and Guild soldiers took the party and the surviving leaders with nets and clubs. Alive, all of them, on an instruction he would not put a name to.

In the cells the Guild asked for one thing: that the city be given up. Harald has not agreed. [[Orgrim]] and Grukk One-Tusk refused to Yennen's face. [[Tarod]] walked the row of iron doors while they thought about it, dragging [[Vorgra]] along them.

## Under the Mountain

Yennen did not stay the Guild's man. He came to the resting place under the mountain with Aran, a Guild soldier who had defected with him, and with two who had not, and Aran killed them. *"Our defection will only go unnoticed briefly. He is old and cunning."*

At the oasis beyond, Guild soldiers rang a golden gong and held Yennen at blade-point. *"He is coming! You are all doomed!"* Otters and warriors in heavy armour fought for the Guild there, and three golden golems came out of the passages and walked into the river. The one they meant came through the southern wall and killed Yennen, his nephew. [[Tarod]] watched the Orks hold their ground and called it fleeing.
