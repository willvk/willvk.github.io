(also referred as Norsemen or the Men of the North)

[[Humans]] from the Middens region are collectively referred to as Norse or Norsemen.

Under [[Ivar|Ivar the Terrible]] they were one tribe. Ivar is dead, and the tribe did not outlive him. [[Harald]], the son he claimed, leads now, with [[Balder]] beside him as Steward and Advisor. The Norse are divided. Only the rivertowns of [[Yorvik]] and Cattegut still answer Harald's call.
