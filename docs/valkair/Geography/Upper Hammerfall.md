Upper Hammerfall is the ancient seat of the [[Elves]], but they were displaced and new leadership took hold. [[The Pact]] held the power here, and the three districts were divided between the [[Ork]] and [[Norse]] tribes and the Palace, which seated the Pact and their close retinue. Few others were permitted passage to Upper Hammerfall, though The Guild and the mysterious Chosen held small patches of influence as reward for their part in taking Hammerfall.

The architecture here is sharp, curved and beautiful. Every rock fits neatly alongside its neighbour, and every vine bends to support both the beauty and the grace of each building. Roads curl elegantly around the district. The marble and cobblestones are separated by natural flowers and roots which match the height and soften the footfalls upon it. All around, nature and beauty intermingle, an awe-inspiring and intimidating presence that seems to loom over its new inhabitants.

## Under the Pact

The [[Norse]] who served under [[Ivar|Jarl Ivar]], known by his enemies as "the terrible", paid no heed to the towering mountains and elven structures. They had paid the blood price and conquered this city. Yes, they relied on alliances with others, but this city was a trophy, and it was theirs. None of the others could have claimed Hammerfall without Ivar's leadership. Victory brought new challenges. The streets needed maintaining where the rivers used to wash the passages clean, and allies had to be kept in check. To the Norse, the strong shelter, the artifacts of the elves and the fortifications they now enjoyed were worth untold riches.

The [[Ork]] warbands served under [[Orgrim]] and still lusted for blood. They were satiated with their lot for a time, but Orks have never been known to play nicely with others for long. *This treaty with the petulant humans grows tiresome. The humans are weak and far too merciful. They outnumber us two to one, yet none can stand against the wrath of the Horde. Why were any of the pointy-ears allowed to live? Why do we feed our enemies? This treaty cannot last, and when it falls, Doomhammer will be ready to hold the hill with the skulls of his foes once more.*

## After the Pact

It did not last. [[The Speaker]] shed his disguise and loosed the Blood Elves on the council, and Ivar died in the massacre. The Pact dissolved in daylight. On the night [[Hannah]] died, [[Yennen]] stepped forward with Guild soldiers at his back and said that Hammerfall passed into Guild administration, effective that night.
