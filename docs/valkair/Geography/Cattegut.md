A Norse rivertown, larger than its neighbour [[Yorvik]], and the seat of [[Ivar|Jarl Ivar]]. From here Ivar united the Norse tribes north of [[Hammerfall]] into a single host. [[Balder]] joined Cattegut on its raids against the towns to the east, and came home from them with [[Ygritte]], until Ivar took her.

Since Ivar's death the [[Norse]] are divided. Cattegut is one of only two rivertowns that still answer [[Harald]]'s call.
