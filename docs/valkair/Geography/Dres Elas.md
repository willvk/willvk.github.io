The name of Hammerfall in Elven times. It was the seat of the [[Thrandurassil]] [[Elves]] and homeland of [[Caerdan]]. The city lost the name when [[The Pact]] took it.

In spring the elven noble families of Dres Elas gathered to offer willing sacrifices to [[Freya]] and ask her for children and blessing, at Galanthas, the Flower Gate. It lies beneath the Hollow Coin in [[Richeri]].
