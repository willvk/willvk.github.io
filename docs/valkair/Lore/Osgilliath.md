# Osgilliath

*The Destroyer. The Fiend Below. Lord of the Depths.*

A pit fiend and a lord of the depths of Hel. His people were few. One of them, a hooded servitor, found [[Hannah]] when she had a following of cutthroats and cowards, and promised her the one thing she wanted. Power.

She cut off her own arms on a black altar, and the pit answered, filling her with molten fire and burning claws. Hannah Half-Elven died on a cross. Hannah the Forsaken came down from it, and the blood she fed her followers made the Blood Elves. The elves call those whose blood he has defiled [[Rethra'kael]]: bound through sacrifice, demonic pacts and profane rebirth. [[The Chosen]] were his.

His temple lay beneath Hammerfall, past the elven tombs. When Hannah died, it began to come down around the party, and they heard the stone settling behind them all the way out.
